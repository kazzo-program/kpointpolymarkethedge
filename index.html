COMPLETE USER INSTRUCTIONS // STEP-BY-STEP BLUEPRINT

How to Lose ~20p on Bet 1 & Guarantee ~70%+ Profit on Bet 2

Follow this exact 2-phase formula every time a bookmaker offers a "Bet £10, Get £10 / £30 Free Bet" promotion.

STEP 01

The Qualifying Bet (Lose Only ~20p)

Your first bet uses your own cash just to unlock the bookmaker's Free Bet promotion. You aren't trying to gamble here — you want to break almost completely even (losing only ~15p to 25p in market spread).

🔍 Exact Odds to Look For on Bet 1:

  • 1. Look for LOW Odds (1.50 to 2.20): Check the promo terms for the minimum odds allowed (usually 1.50 [1/2] or 2.00 [Evens]). Pick a team or selection right near that minimum. Why? Because low odds keep your exchange liability small (only ~£5 to £12 needed) and minimise commission losses.
  • 2. Keep Back & Lay Odds Within 0.02 to 0.04: For example, if the Bookmaker offers Back Odds of 2.00, look for Exchange Lay Odds of 2.00, 2.02, or 2.04 (or in 2-Bookie mode, two opposite outcomes like Over/Under both at 1.98).
  • 3. Target a 97.5%+ Match Rating: At 2.00 Back / 2.02 Lay on a £10 stake, your guaranteed loss is just 15p to 20p. Think of that 20p as buying a £10 or £30 Free Bet token!
✔ DO: Bet on high-liquidity football/tennis matches where Back & Lay odds are razor-tight.
✖ DON'T: Use high odds (like 6.00) for your Qualifying Bet — spreads are wider and you'll lose £1+ unnecessarily.
STEP 02

The Free Bet Hedge (Guarantee ~70%–80% Profit)

Once Bet 1 settles, the bookmaker gives you a Free Bet (SNR — Stake Not Returned). Because the bookmaker keeps the free bet stake and only pays the winnings, higher odds give you a much higher guaranteed profit!

🚀 Exact Odds to Look For on Bet 2:

  • 1. Look for HIGH Odds (4.50 to 7.00): Why? On a £10 Free Bet at 2.00 odds, your bookie winnings are only £10, leaving you with just ~£4.80 (48%) after hedging. At 5.00 odds, your bookie winnings are £40.00 — meaning the unreturned £10 stake is only a tiny fraction of the payout, locking in ~£7.55 (75.5% guaranteed profit)!
  • 2. Keep the High-Odds Spread Tight (Within 0.15 to 0.30): Ideal examples: Back 4.50 / Lay 4.65 (74.0% profit), Back 5.00 / Lay 5.20 (75.4% profit), or Back 6.00 / Lay 6.25 (78.4% profit).
  • 3. If Using 2 Bookmakers (No Exchange): Place your Free Bet on an underdog at Bookie 1 (e.g., Odds 4.20 to 5.50) and hedge with cash on the heavy favorite / Double Chance at Bookie 2 (e.g., Odds 1.25 to 1.36).
✔ DO: Check your Exchange account balance can cover the Liability (usually 3x–4x the Free Bet stake at 5.0 odds).
✖ DON'T: Place your Free Bet at low odds like 1.80 or 2.00 — you'll throw away ~25% of your guaranteed cash!
RULE 01

Understand "Lay" vs "Back"

Back = betting FOR an outcome to happen (at the Bookmaker).
Lay = betting AGAINST that exact same outcome (at Betfair, Smarkets, or Matchbook) so every possible match result is 100% covered.

RULE 02

Why 4.50–6.50 is the Sweet Spot

Odds above 7.50 can push profit toward 80%, but require a large exchange balance for liability. Odds between 4.50 and 6.00 easily beat the 70%–77% target while keeping liability manageable.

RULE 03

Football Draws & Underdogs Shine

Premier League / Champions League Match Result Draws or away underdogs often sit right at 4.20–5.50 with ultra-tight exchange spreads (e.g. 4.80 Back / 4.90 Lay).

RULE 04

Never Hedge on the Same Bookie

Always place your Qualifying/Free Bet at Bookmaker A and your Hedge Bet at a Betting Exchange (or Bookmaker B). Never bet both sides of a market on the same bookmaker account.
