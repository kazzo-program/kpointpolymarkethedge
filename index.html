How the Quant Hedging Engine Extracts Daily Profit from Polymarket

STRATEGY 01 // DUTCH_ARB

Multi-Bracket Sum-of-NO Arbitrage

In multi-outcome Polymarket events (such as Federal Reserve rate decisions, election margins, or price brackets), mutually exclusive outcomes must sum to 100% at expiration. When retail inflows push the sum of implied probabilities above 103%+, buying weighted NO shares or pairing the primary bracket against complementary field brackets locks a combined unit cost below $1.00.

Σ Cost = P(Leg 1) + P(Leg 2) < $1.00 ⇒ Locked Yield = (1 / Σ Cost) - 1
STRATEGY 02 // SPREAD_STRADDLE

Delta-Neutral Maker Bid-Ask Straddle

Polymarket charges 0 bps maker fees on resting limit orders in the Central Limit Order Book (CLOB). On high-volume daily contracts trading between 35¢ and 65¢ with a 2.5¢–5.0¢ bid-ask spread, posting passive limit bids on both YES and NO captures both sides of the book for $0.95–$0.97 combined cost per $1.00 payout.

Bid(YES) + Bid(NO) = $0.962 ⇒ Guaranteed +$0.038 (+3.95%) at Settlement
STRATEGY 03 // CONVEX_COLLAR

Asymmetric Convexity + NO Anchor Collar

When an underpriced catalyst contract trades below 35¢ with positive orderbook skew, unhedged buyers face binary decay. By sizing an anchor NO hedge so its settlement payout covers 100% of total deployed capital (Zero-Loss Floor mode), the trader holds free upside convexity on YES without principal risk.

Shares(NO) = Total Capital ÷ $1.00 ⇒ Downside = $0.00 | Upside = Pure Alpha
STRATEGY 04 // THETA_PAIR

High-Probability Yield + Tail Protection

Late-stage contracts trading at 65¢–90¢ offer strong convergence yield (theta decay toward $1.00), but carry tail-event wipeout risk. Pairing the high-probability favorite with deeply discounted counter-bracket NO shares caps variance and elevates the portfolio Sharpe ratio above 2.8+.

Stake(Leg 1) = Bankroll × [P2 / (P1 + P2)] • Stake(Leg 2) = Bankroll × [P1 / (P1 + P2)]
